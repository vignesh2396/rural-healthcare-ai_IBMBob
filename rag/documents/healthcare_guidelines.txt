SECTION 1: HEALTHCARE FACILITY TYPES IN INDIA
===============================================

Sub-Centre (SC):
- Lowest level of contact between the health system and community
- Provides basic maternal and child health, family planning, nutrition
- Staffed by: ANM (Auxiliary Nurse Midwife) and Male Health Worker
- Serves: 5,000 population (plains) / 3,000 (tribal/hilly areas)
- Services: Immunization, ANC, delivery care, family planning, basic first aid

Primary Health Centre (PHC):
- First point of contact between community and medical officer
- Provides curative, preventive, and promotive healthcare
- Staffed by: Medical Officer, Health Inspector, ANM
- Serves: 30,000 population (plains) / 20,000 (tribal areas)
- Services: OPD, 6-bed inpatient, maternal health, child health, disease control
- Free medicines: Available under National Free Medicines Initiative

Community Health Centre (CHC):
- Referral unit for Primary Health Centres
- Provides specialist services
- Staffed by: 4 specialists (surgeon, physician, gynecologist, pediatrician)
- Serves: 1,20,000 population
- Services: 30-bed hospital, surgery, obstetrics, radiology, laboratory
- IPHS (Indian Public Health Standards) norms apply

Sub-District/Taluk Hospital:
- Serves as referral for CHCs
- 100-200 beds
- Specialist services, emergency care

District Hospital:
- Apex referral facility in a district
- 200-500 beds or more
- Full specialist services, ICU, blood bank, dialysis

Medical College & Teaching Hospitals:
- Tertiary care
- Full super-specialist services
- Trauma centers, transplant units

SECTION 3: GOVERNMENT HEALTH SCHEMES
======================================

Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY):
- India's largest government-funded health insurance scheme
- Coverage: Up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalization
- Beneficiaries: Bottom 40% of India's population (~10.74 crore families)
- Eligibility: Based on SECC 2011 database (Socio-Economic Caste Census)
- Services: 1,961 treatment packages covering surgery, medical, and day care
- Cashless: Yes, at empanelled hospitals (both government and private)
- Portability: Valid across India
- How to check: Call 14555 or visit pmjay.gov.in

National Health Mission (NHM):
- Free OPD consultations at government facilities
- Free essential medicines (NLEM - National List of Essential Medicines)
- Free diagnostics under National Free Diagnostics Service Initiative
- Free ambulance services (108/102)
- Free maternal care: ANC, delivery, postnatal care
- Free child health services: Immunization, RBSK, nutrition

Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY):
- Accident insurance: Rs 2 lakh coverage
- Premium: Rs 20/year
- Age: 18-70 years

Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY):
- Life insurance: Rs 2 lakh
- Premium: Rs 436/year

State-Specific Schemes (Maharashtra examples):
- Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY)
- Coverage: Rs 1.5 lakh for non-PMJAY families
- Combined with PMJAY for eligible families

SECTION 4: DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR HEALTHCARE ACCESS
=====================================================

For Government Hospital/PHC/CHC:
- Aadhaar Card (proof of identity and address)
- Ration Card (for BPL/APL classification)
- Ayushman Bharat Golden Card / PMJAY Card
- State Health Card (if applicable)
- Voter ID (alternative identity proof)
- Previous medical records, prescriptions, test reports

For Emergency Care:
- No documents required for emergency treatment at government hospitals
- Emergency stabilization is provided regardless of documents (as per law)

SECTION 5: ASHA WORKERS AND COMMUNITY HEALTH SUPPORT
======================================================

ASHA (Accredited Social Health Activist):
- Village-level health worker
- One ASHA per 1,000 population
- Roles: Health promotion, escort patients to facility, maternal health support
- Can help with: PMJAY card verification, facility referrals, transport arrangement
- Provides: Information about government schemes, free medicines, immunization

How to contact ASHA:
- Available in every village
- Contact through ANM or PHC
- Contact through Panchayat office

ANM (Auxiliary Nurse Midwife):
- Subcenter level health worker
- Maternal & child health services
- Immunization, ANC, family planning
- First point of referral for complex cases

SECTION 6: TELEMEDICINE GUIDELINES
====================================

Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (India, 2020):
- Legal framework: Telemedicine Practice Guidelines under IMC Act, 1956
- Permitted: Video, audio, and text consultations
- OPD consultations: Can be done via telemedicine
- Prescriptions: Can be issued via telemedicine (Schedule H1 drugs excluded)

National Teleconsultation Service (eSanjeevani):
- Free teleconsultation service
- esanjeevaniopd.in or eSanjeevani app
- Connects patients with government doctors
- Available in all states

SECTION 7: PATIENT RIGHTS IN INDIA
====================================

Right to Emergency Care:
- All hospitals (government and private) must provide emergency treatment
- Cannot be denied based on inability to pay
- Clinical Establishments Act 2010

Right to Information:
- Right to know diagnosis, treatment options, risks, costs
- Informed consent required before major procedures

Right to Privacy:
- Medical records are confidential
- Patient data protected under IT Act

SECTION 8: RURAL HEALTHCARE CHALLENGES
========================================

Key Challenges Identified:
1. Distance: Average distance to PHC in rural areas: 4-8 km
2. Transport: Limited public transport, high cost of private vehicles
3. Awareness: Low awareness of available government services
4. Specialists: Only 1 specialist per 100,000 population in rural India
5. Medicine availability: Stock-outs at PHC/CHC level
6. Diagnostic services: Limited lab facilities at peripheral level
7. Language: Communication barriers in tribal areas
8. Financial: Out-of-pocket expenditure still high despite schemes
9. Time loss: Long waiting times at district hospitals
10. Fragmented referral: Poor coordination between PHC/CHC/District Hospital

Solutions Being Implemented:
- Digital health records (ABHA - Ayushman Bharat Health Account)
- Telemedicine expansion (eSanjeevani)
- Mobile health units
- ASHA incentive programs
- Free diagnostics and medicines
- Emergency transport (108 free ambulance)
